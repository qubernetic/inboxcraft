# 343

## Changelog
### Added
- `event.create` and `event.update` take an `alert`: `"none"` or the minutes before the start, at most a week,
  written into the event as one display alarm; an update moves only the event's first display or sound alarm and
  keeps every other alarm. `"none"` takes the display and sound alarms off, so the local alert no longer rings at
  the default ten minutes either. `event.list` says each event's alert (#343)

## Roadmap
- Calendar: an alert of its own on an event, written as its alarm over CalDAV (#343)

## Gaps
- The event editor's Alert field is held by the form and saved through `event.create` and `event.update`, but the
  full editor and the window that draw it are not built yet; the small editor has no Alert field, as drawn (#343).
- A new event's Alert says the default lead it rings with (10 min before, 1 day before an all-day event) where the
  design's new-event frame says None (#343).
