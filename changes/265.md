# 265

## Changelog
### Added
- The time picker of Snooze, Send later and a follow-up reminder lists "This weekend" and "Next week" after
  "Tomorrow morning", in the engine's order and at the engine's times (Next week at 09:00) (#265)

### Changed
- The calendar counts weeks by the week rule of `week.set`: the week view runs from the week's first day, the
  month view and the mini month start their columns on it (the mini month's letters follow), and `week <n>` is the
  number the rule gives at the year's turn. With the default rule (Monday, the first four-day week) nothing looks
  different (#265)

## Gaps
- The window reads the week rule when the calendar reads its days (a step, a view, a sync), not yet once a frame,
  and does not yet keep it on disk: the week set through `week.set` shows at the calendar's next read and is
  Monday again at the next start (#265).
- With Saturday as the first day of the week, This weekend and Next week are the same moment on most days; the
  time picker shows both, as the engine gives them (#265).

## Revisions
- docs/gaps.md | minor | The week rule's reading each frame and its keeping; the two presets on the same Saturday (#265)
