## Changelog
### Added
- `ui.home.open` (`safe`, no parameters) is an engine command: the control channel, the CLI in the running app and
  an agent can show Home. An agent's call is passed over as an agent's `ui.settings.open` is (the account form holds
  what the owner typed, a key is being given) and while the owner edits an event in the window's event editor; the
  owner's own key or press in the same frame wins, and of an agent's calls of `ui.home.open` and `ui.settings.open`
  only the newest acts in a frame. On a store it shows nothing, and its answer says so with `"window": false` (#399)

### Fixed
- Home's failing banner says what fails, from `sync.status` and `account.list`: "Offline since 14:10: what you see is
  what this computer holds." when no account is signed in after a sync, the account that refused the sign-in by its
  name (or how many did) with "open account", and the sends the server refused, the first by its recipient, with
  "open outbox" and "Nothing is lost.", in the design file's words (#399)
- Home's failing banner wraps its words onto two lines, as the design file draws it, where it cut them on one; it is
  72 high, and the tiles stand at 280 (#399)

## Gaps
- Home (#399): the banner says "offline since" only after a sync of this run: before the first one (and with an
  account still connecting) it says nothing of it, and the status line says offline. Of the failures that stop an
  account until the owner acts, only a refused sign-in is in the banner; an unanswered sign-in, a missing password or
  a certificate refused are the status line's.
