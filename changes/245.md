# 245

## Changelog
### Security
- Send again on a refused send no longer sends a draft that changed since it was refused, by an agent or anyone,
  without the owner seeing it: `outbox.retry` refuses a changed draft unless it carries the `seen` mark
  `outbox.list` gave the draft as it still is, and the outbox row's first press asks, with every recipient and what
  changed, and only a second press ("Send as it is now") sends it (#245)
### Changed
- `outbox.list` gives each item's `cc` and `bcc` beside `to`, and for a refused one what `changed` since it was
  sent and its `seen` mark; an item an older build kept counts as changed (`unknown`) (#245)
- The outbox row lists To, Cc and Bcc on its one line ("To Odile · Cc Ann · Bcc Béla", at most 20 of each named
  and the rest counted), and a refused send that changed says so before the server's reply ("Changed since it was
  refused: Bcc, text") (#245)
- The outbox row's buttons answer the owner's own press only: a held Enter or Space repeating does not press them
  again (#245)

## Gaps
- The outbox row's Cc and Bcc on its line, "Changed since it was refused: …" before the reply and the button "Send
  as it is now" are not drawn by the design file, whose outbox row has To and the reply only (#245)
