# 256

## Changelog
### Added
- `mail.why` (`safe`): why a message stands where it does, at most four reasons, most specific first (back from a
  snooze while it is in the inbox, or snoozed until, the grouped inbox's rule, the owner's choices for the sender,
  the screener, the thread, else the folder), each an i18n key and its values, never text of the message (#256)

## Roadmap
- Qubernetic look: `mail.why`, the reasons for the reader's why line, from the triage's own decisions (#256)

## Gaps
- `mail.why` cannot say when the owner let a sender in or moved a message, nor that a move was the owner's: the
  engine keeps no date for those decisions and no history of moves. Like `mail.thread`, it reads every message of
  every account to find the thread. It is not offered as an MCP tool until the owner has decided whether a client
  may read the group and its rule, the owner's choice for the sender, the snooze times and whether the owner wrote
  in the thread; it runs through the CLI and the control channel as a `safe` command (#256).
