# 147

## Changelog
### Added
- The calendar screen shows the calendars of every account in four views, Day, Week, Month and Agenda, picked by
  pills over the view, with steps back and on and Today. Day and week lay the hours out with the timed events by
  their minutes, side by side where they overlap, under a lane of blocks over whole days, and a line at the time
  now; the month runs blocks over several days across its weeks, with "+N more" for what does not fit; the agenda
  lists the 30 days from today, day by day (#147)
- The calendar's side panel: a mini month that puts the view on a day, a box per calendar that shows or hides its
  events, and the details of a picked event with both dates and its times, the event's own zone labelled with its
  times when its clock differs from the computer's, the place, the description, busy or free, private, the repeat
  and the guests with their answers (#147)
- The sample mailbox has three invented calendars with events around the current week; `ui_shot` draws the week,
  month, agenda and day in light and dark (`"calendar_view"`, `"event"`) (#147)
- The Previous, Next and Globe icons in both icon sets (#147)

## Roadmap
- E4.4 in the window: the day, week, month and agenda views with overlaps side by side and blocks over several
  days, the mini month, the calendars' boxes and the details with the event's own zone (#147)

## Gaps
- The calendar's views read the computer's clock as the engine knows it, which is UTC until a front end hands the
  engine the computer's offsets; the line at the time now moves only when the calendar is read again (a view, a
  step, a sync). Weeks start on Monday and the hours have no setting for the start of the day (#147).
- An event over more than one day shows as a block over whole days in the day, week and month views, even when it
  has times (a night from 22:00 to 02:00); its times show in its details. Overlapping timed events share their
  group's width in equal columns, however short the overlap (#147).
- The calendar's views have no keys of their own (steps, views, today), and an event picked by the keyboard is
  reached by Tab only. Events are not created, changed or deleted in the window yet (K4); the details have no Edit
  or Delete (#147).
- Hiding a calendar lasts until the window closes; it is not kept between runs (#147).
