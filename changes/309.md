# 309

## Changelog
### Added
- The status line at the window's foot says how things stand: the worst state first (an account's failure with the
  count of the others, offline, the sync's progress while it runs, or when the last sync ended), then the accounts,
  the outbox (and when a held send goes) and the next of today's events, with the keys that work now at its right.
  A failing account's "open account" opens the accounts (#309)

### Changed
- What was a toast is a message in the status line: an act's Undo, the send countdown with its Undo, a discard from
  Home with its Undo, the composer's notices and the command errors. One message shows at a time; notices and
  errors wait their turn (at most eight), an error stays until dismissed, and an Undo works by its key only while its
  message shows (#309)
- Undo is offered for five seconds, the discard from Home waits as long, and a notice shows for four (#309)
- The error banner over the panes is gone: a command's error is the status line's (#309)
- Under the command line's results nothing in the status line runs, a send countdown's and a discard's Undo
  included (#309)

### Fixed
- A message with Undo no longer covers the reader's Reply, Reply all and Forward, nor takes their presses: the
  status line is a strip of its own (#296, #309)

## Roadmap

## Gaps
- Status line (#309): offline says "offline" with no time since and no "try again"; an account's failure names the
  account and the engine's sentence ("Studio: sign-in refused" in the frames), and the frame's "Personal synced 14:02"
  is "synced 14:02" for all accounts; the syncing note is the share done ("54 %"), not the account's name.
- Status line (#309): the messages keep the window's words ("Marked as done", where the frames write "done · Draft
  logo for the bakery"); the send countdown has no "send now"; the error's "open log" and the problems' "open log"
  are drawn coming soon; the error's "dismiss" has no key, so no "dismiss" hint stands at the right.
- Status line (#309): the keys at its right are next, previous, done, snooze and commands, each only while it works;
  under 1280 and 980 the line does not drop facts and keys yet (the widths).
- Status line (#309): the message shows at once, without the frame's motion; its bar and seconds change once a second.
