# 275

## Changelog
### Added
- The character field and the emblem as a module of the window's crate, not yet behind any screen: a grid of
  8 × 15 point cells, about 5 in 100 holding a `·` in `faint`, the same pattern on every launch; the field's
  cells within 60 points of the pointer flip to code glyphs and cool down within 200 ms (at the reduced level
  only Home's field, within 30 points; at off still dots); an emblem the caller hands in, drawn at its one size
  only where it fits whole, its cells flipping and settling back under the pointer at full and reduced. The
  field takes no click, key or focus and asks for frames only while a cell cools (#275)
- A gallery page of the field at 100 % and 130 % text (`ui_shot` key `character_field`), and `ui_bench --field`:
  a window and a 4K screen of the field with the pointer sweeping (#275)

## Roadmap
- Qubernetic look: the character field and the emblem as a module, with its gallery page and bench (#275)

## Gaps
- The character field is not shown yet: Home, the first start, the cover and the panes' empty areas do not use it
  (#275)
- The emblem's own character layout is not in the code: the field draws whatever grid it is handed, and its gallery
  page shows a plain place holder of the emblem's 76 × 40 cells (#275)
- At 130 % text the field's cells are 8 × 20 points (the width stays, the height follows the text to the whole
  point) and the dot keeps its 13.33 point size; the design draws the field at 100 % only (#275)
- Which of the field's cells flip under the pointer, how long each takes to cool and the glyph it flips to are
  fixed hashes, not chance: about 45 in 100 of the cells within reach flip, the nearest most often, and about half
  of the emblem's (#275)
