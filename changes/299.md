# 299

## Changelog
### Added
- Home as a page module: the brief of today (the date, a greeting with the owner's first name, a
  sentence of what waits, four count tiles of one height, the rest of today's agenda with at most three lines, the
  two newest drafts, two buttons), the form before the first account (what InboxCraft is, two buttons and the four
  steps adding an account takes) and a failing banner in the sentence's place when the server refused a send. It is
  built from the mailbox through `safe` commands only and gives back what was pressed without running it. It is
  drawn on a page of its own for `ui_shot` (`"home": "<state>"`) in its five states (#299)
- `compose.list` takes `limit` (1 to 1000; more is cut to 1000, 0 or anything not a whole number is refused) and
  answers only that many of the newest drafts; its answer gains `total`, how many drafts there are in all, with or
  without `limit` (#299)

## Gaps
- Home (#299): the emblem's place holds the character field alone; the emblem and the two wordmarks under it are
  not drawn until the project owner decides the mark's licence. The rule that the emblem leaves whole where it does
  not fit is built and tested with a placeholder shape.
- Home (#299): no source yet for "offline since" and for an account that refused the sign-in, so the failing banner
  shows only refused sends; the counts' fourth tile turns to "not sent" then. "Back today" counts the snoozed mail
  of one page of `mail.list` (the engine's list limit). An agenda line's detail says where and how long, never "in
  58 min". The greeting's sentence is written from the counts and the next event, plainer than the file's words.
- Home (#299): the failing banner takes one line of text and cuts the rest of its words with one `…` ("… Studio
  refused the sign…" in the gallery's longest sentence), where the file wraps it onto two lines; it is 54 high
  against 72, so the tiles stand at 262 against the file's 280 in `home/everything-failing`. The banner is the one
  onboarding shows too. Home shows two drafts where `home-drafts/default` draws
  three (the page frames draw two). The file's "MISSING UI" note on the drafts' block is not drawn.
- Home (#299): where the brief does not fit, the emblem's place goes first, then the brief takes the whole width
  under 600 and scrolls when it is taller than the body; the file draws neither.

- Home (#299): the greeting names the first word of the account's sending name, so a name written family name first
  is greeted by the family name; there is no setting for it yet. The date, the greeting and "the rest of today" go by
  the window's clock, which is UTC like the rest of the app until a front end hands the engine the computer's zone.
