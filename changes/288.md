## Changelog
### Added
- Home's sets, not yet behind a screen: the count tile (a count over its label, in the tone's colour, `muted` at 0,
  capped at `9 999+`, a button that takes its row's height), the block (a hairline box with its section label and a hairline over its content, a
  button when asked, selected on `stage`) and the block line (why a block holds nothing, or the small link to the
  whole list, which returns its press). They are drawn on a gallery page of their own for `ui_shot`
  (`"home_sets": true`) in light and dark (#288)

## Gaps
- Home's sets (#288): no screen uses them yet; Home comes in a later slice. The sheets give no pressed state: a
  pressed count tile keeps its hover fill and a pressed block its `muted` edge. A count tile's label takes at most
  two lines and a block line's empty text at most three, each cut with `…` after them. At 130 % text a tile, a block
  head and a more line take the scaled heights (98, 46, 42) while their paddings stay, so the room the scaling
  adds stands under the text. A count is written as the index writes its counts, groups of three apart by a narrow
  no-break space.
