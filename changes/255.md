# 255

## Changelog
### Added
- `sync.status` (`safe`, no parameters) answers how syncing stands over every account: `state`, the first that
  holds of `failing` (an account stopped until the owner acts: a refused or unanswered sign-in, no password, a
  certificate or encryption refused), `offline` (no account signed in, connecting counts as not yet), `failing`
  (a signed-in account's last connection or job failed, a body fetch aside), `syncing` (a round has folders left,
  or a signed-in account has not ended a round yet) and `synced` (always with no account); `done` and `of` (the
  folder syncs of the rounds under way); `last_synced` (the newest end of a round that ended with nothing failed,
  ISO 8601 UTC, or `null`) and `failing_accounts`. It runs in the CLI, headless (where no worker runs, so an
  account reads as `offline`) and over the control channel (#255)
- The workers' events `SyncProgress` (folder syncs done of all in the account's round) and `SyncFinished` (the
  account is current); an end more than five minutes ahead of the clock is dropped and one less far ahead is taken
  as the clock, and an event for an account the session does not hold is dropped too. A round's progress ends with
  the round, with its connection going offline and with changed settings (#255)
- Each account's worker counts the folder syncs it holds as a round: it tells the round's progress when it starts
  and right after each folder ends, synced or failed, and its end when no job and no connection failed during it;
  a resync of the watched folder tells an end too, unless something failed since the last sign-in or the last
  round that ended so. It counts the jobs it has, with no request to the server (#255)
### Changed
- `account.list`'s `failure` also ends when a round of the account ends with nothing failed, not only when the
  account is online again, and a body fetch that failed no longer sets it (#255)

## Roadmap
- `sync.status`: the sync state, progress and last sync for the status line (#255)

## Gaps
- `sync.status` is not an MCP tool: the MCP server's tools are a fixed list (#255).
- A connection that drops under a job is signed in again at once, and `sync.status` reads `offline` (or `failing`
  beside a signed-in account) for that sign-in: the worker tells the drop and the new attempt, nothing that marks
  it as brief (#255).
