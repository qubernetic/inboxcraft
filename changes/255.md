# 255

## Changelog
### Added
- `sync.status` (`safe`, no parameters) answers how syncing stands over every account: `state` (`offline` when no
  account is connected, `failing` when an account's last connection or job failed, `syncing` while a sync round has
  folders left, else `synced`; the worst wins, and with no account it is `synced`), `done` and `of` (the folder
  syncs of the rounds told, each `done` at most its `of`), `last_synced` (the newest end of a sync round, ISO 8601
  UTC, or `null`) and `failing_accounts`. It runs in the CLI, headless (where no worker runs, so an account reads
  as `offline`) and over the control channel (#255)
- The workers' events `SyncProgress` (folder syncs done of all in the account's round) and `SyncFinished` (when a
  round ended with every folder synced); an end of a round more than a day ahead of the clock is dropped, and an
  event for an account the session does not hold too (#255)
### Changed
- `account.list`'s `failure` also ends when a sync round of the account ends with every folder synced, not only
  when the account is online again (#255)

## Roadmap
- `sync.status`: the sync state, progress and last sync for the status line (#255)

## Gaps
- `sync.status` is not an MCP tool: the MCP server's tools are a fixed list (#255).
