# 260

## Changelog
### Changed
- Detect tries more ways, in this order: autoconfig at the domain (and the ISPDB when it is on), SRV records, the
  provider domains of the domain's MX hosts (their autoconfig over HTTPS, then `mail.`, `imap.` and `smtp.` of
  them), then `mail.`, `imap.` and `smtp.` of the domain itself. A host made up from a domain is tried only on the
  registered ports: IMAP 993 with TLS, then 143 with STARTTLS; submission 465 with TLS, then 587 with STARTTLS; never
  without encryption and never port 25 (#260)
- Detect proposes only servers that answered its probe: each candidate is connected to, encrypted (TLS, or STARTTLS
  first), and must offer a sign-in the app can do; no user name or password is sent. It proposes the first IMAP and
  the first submission server that answered, and stops once it has both, after 24 probes or after 120 seconds. What
  it probed stands in the list of what it asked, and each probe that failed with its cause. Common host names are no
  longer proposed unchecked: when nothing answers, nothing is proposed (#260)
### Fixed
- An address at a domain whose mail a provider hosts, where the domain answers nothing on the mail ports, is found
  through the provider named by the domain's MX records (`mail.<provider>`) (#260)

## Gaps
- Detect's provider domains come from the MX hosts without a public suffix list: the parents of each MX host,
  dropping the first label while at least two labels remain and never above the MX domain of the rule of thumb,
  at most two of them (`mx1.mail.example.net` gives `mail.example.net` and `example.net`). A provider whose mail
  hosts are not under its MX hosts' domain is not found this way (#260).
- Detect proposes only servers that answered its probe, so a network that blocks the mail ports finds nothing, even
  a server the provider's autoconfig file names; the account form and its check stay the way to enter it. The probe
  connects to names made up from the domain and the MX hosts, so the system looks those names up (#260).
- Detect's caps are checked before each lookup and probe; one that has started runs to its own timeouts (10 s to
  connect, 15 s for each read and write in the app), so a run can last somewhat past its 120 seconds. Cancelling the
  form drops the run's answer; the run itself goes on in the background until it ends or reaches its cap (#260).
- Detect proposes one IMAP and one submission server, the first that answered, not a list to choose from. The form
  has no words of its own yet for a server found through the MX hosts' provider: it shows it as a common server
  name, a guess to confirm (#260).
- Servers named by SRV records and autoconfig files keep the port they name (port 25 is left out); only the hosts
  Detect makes up from a domain are limited to 993, 143, 465 and 587 (#260).
