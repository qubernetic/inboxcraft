## Changelog
### Added
- Settings, Sending: "manage templates →" opens the Templates page, a group per account with a row per template
  (its name, its subject and text on one line, edit and delete). New template and edit open a form on the page
  (account, name, subject, text; Save and Cancel) that says when Save replaces another template of the same name;
  a renamed or moved template leaves no copy under its old name. Delete asks in place, says that it cannot be
  undone, and deletes only on the press of "Delete template"; Esc or Cancel keeps it (#317)
- Settings, Sending: "open the list →" opens the Scheduled page, everything that waits for its time, the next first:
  a row per entry with its time, its kind (send, snooze, reminder, alert), the account's bar, its subject and whom it
  names (or how long before its event an alert rings), and cancel. Cancel asks in place, says that it cannot be undone
  and what follows (a send goes back to Drafts), and cancels only on the press of the confirmation's button; Esc or
  "Keep it" keeps it (#317)
- Settings, Privacy: the senders whose remote pictures always load, a row per sender with "ask again", which takes the
  sender off the list at once on its press (its pictures are held again) and says so in the status line; with
  nobody on the list the page says so (#317)
- Settings, Calendar: the "Calendar alerts" switch works: off, no event's alert notifies, and its help says so (#317)
- Settings, Accounts: an account's form has "Remove account…" at the right end of its buttons. It asks in the buttons'
  place, first that it cannot be undone, then what goes from this computer and what stays on the server; only the
  press of the question's "Remove account" removes the account with what this computer keeps of it and takes its
  password out of the system's credential store, and the status line says so. A held Enter or Space never answers
  the question (#317)

### Changed
- `schedule.list` answers, beside each entry's id, time and target, its account, its subject (an alert's event
  title) and who it names (a send's recipients, a snooze's sender, a follow-up's recipient), each one short line
  without control characters; an alert's entry adds its event, its start and how many minutes before it rings (#317)

## Gaps
- Templates page (#317): a delete is confirmed in place, where the row stood, not in the frames' dialog over a scrim;
  it adds "This cannot be undone." because the engine cannot undo `template.delete`.
- Templates page (#317): the groups follow the engine's order of accounts (Personal before Studio in the sample),
  where the frames show Studio first; the form's title is "New template" for a new one, "Edit a template" for an
  edit; the intro and the empty state finish the sentences the frames cut with "…".
- Scheduled page (#317): a cancel is confirmed in place, where the row stood, not in the frames' 480×250 dialog over
  a scrim (no "CONFIRM" label); it says "This cannot be undone." first because the engine keeps no undo for
  `schedule.cancel`, and adds when the entry is due. The intro's second sentence says that cancelling anything but a
  send takes only its time off the schedule (the engine leaves a snoozed message as it is), where the frames' sentence
  goes on about a snooze; the page has no picture of its empty state yet.
- Scheduled page (#317): times within the coming week are a short weekday (`Fri 11:00`), further on the day and month
  (`12 Nov 09:00`); the frames show only today and weekdays.
- Privacy page (#317): a sender's row shows its address alone, in the frames' name column; the frames' name (180 wide,
  strong) and address (424 wide, mono) need a name the engine does not keep for an allowed sender.
- Removing an account (#317): the question stands in place of the form's buttons, not in the frames' 480-wide dialog
  over a scrim (no CONFIRM label, no shadow); the form's title is still "Account settings", without the account's bar
  and name the frame has; the frame's line about the drafts gives their number, the question does not.
