# 232

## Changelog
### Added
- Asset files pinned by sha256 in `.config/assets.toml`, checked by `cargo xtask assets` (#232)
- The InboxCraft marks (app icon in the hicolor sizes, tray icons, marks, wordmark and lockup in SVG, app icon PNGs)
  and the Qubernetic horizontal lockup under `assets/brand/`, copied from the brand repository's tag v0.3.0 and
  pinned; trademarks of Qubernetic, not under MIT or Apache-2.0, and not yet used by the code (#232)
### Changed
- The app's type is IBM Plex Mono for the app's own words and IBM Plex Sans for what people wrote, Regular and
  SemiBold, in twelve styles with their line heights and tracking (labels in capitals), and Audiowide for the
  product names; the fonts are pinned in `.config/assets.toml`. Dates, counts, labels and buttons move onto Plex
  Mono, and rows and controls take the styles' line heights (#232)
- Phosphor Regular is the one icon family and weight: an active or selected icon is the accent colour, not a filled
  glyph; icons are 16 px in controls (#232)
### Removed
- Figtree, Inter, Lucide and the Phosphor Fill and Duotone fonts (#232)

## Revisions
- docs/development.md | minor | Pinned asset files: the manifest, its format and the gate's messages (#232)
- docs/gaps.md | minor | IBM Plex type, its fallbacks and row height; Phosphor Regular only (#232)
