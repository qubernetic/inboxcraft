## Changelog
### Changed
- Home's character field and emblem move while the pointer stands still: the cells within its reach keep flipping
  (a cell that flipped may flip again after 50 to 190 ms, one that did not after 60 ms), a cell the pointer left
  takes a new glyph every 70 to 230 ms until it has cooled, and the emblem twinkles by itself, about 7.5 cells a
  second, half of them cyan, each for 250 to 750 ms, starting a quarter second after Home is shown; at the
  reduced level only on Home, at off nothing moves. The field asks for a frame when its next change is due, not
  sooner: about 30 a second while the emblem twinkles with the pointer away, none on a pane or Home without the
  emblem at rest (#418)
