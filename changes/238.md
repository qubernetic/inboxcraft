# 238

## Changelog
### Added
- Settings: the sections Calendar, Privacy and About of the design, and the planned AI and Plugins, each with its
  page; what is not built yet on them, and on the other pages (open or write a message in a window, Undo send set to
  Off, the templates and the scheduled list, each connection's state, removing an account, setting up a phone), is
  drawn coming soon (#238)
### Changed
- The settings sheet takes the design's layout: a column of sections, 232 wide, with "Settings" and how many there
  are, beside the open section's page in a column 720 wide; a page is where it is (`[ 04 ] SETTINGS · APPEARANCE`),
  its title and a sentence, then its groups, each a section label over settings rows; the preview is gone (#238)
- Appearance holds Look (mode, reduce motion, text size) and Layout (the inbox view, where a message opens and is
  written, the window frame); the inbox view moves there from Triage and its views are named Grouped, Smart and
  List (#238)
- The settings sheet keeps the keyboard: `Tab` goes round its controls only, and when it closes the keyboard goes
  back to the control it opened from (#238)
### Fixed
- No way out of the settings sheet loses what was typed: a signature typed and not saved stays until it is saved,
  and the add or edit form of an account, left by Escape, Close, a press beside the sheet, another section, Back or
  the rail or the command line, comes back as typed with Add account or any account's row, never dropped by asking
  for another form (Cancel lets it go); its password stays in memory only (#238)

## Gaps
- The settings are still a sheet over the window, with a Close button the design does not have (settings are a place
  of the window in the design); in the app's own frame the sheet starts under the window's controls, which stay
  pressable above it (#238)
- Motion is the "Reduce motion" switch, not the design's three levels; the window frame offers today's three frames
  (Title bar, Top row, Strip), not the design's own and system frame (#238)
- The inbox views carry the design's names over today's views: Grouped is the one list under headings, Smart the
  cards, List the one list by date; the app still starts on Smart, the design on Grouped (#238)
- Undo send set to Off is a coming-soon row of its own, "Send at once", not an Off option of the hold's choice (#238)
- About shows its facts as coming soon, without the marks, the lockup and the emblem of the design (#238)
- The rows of the settings sections and their selected row are the folder pane's rows, not yet the design's mailbox
  row (#238)
