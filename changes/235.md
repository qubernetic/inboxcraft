# 235

## Changelog
### Added
- The shared rows and blocks of the design as components: the list row (its hover fill by the ground it stands on,
  the current row's bar, the line under it, the focus edge inside it), the banner in its six kinds, the empty state,
  the progress line with its bar of ten characters, the settings row and the coming-soon one, a step of an account's
  check, and the facts of a message with their details (#235)
### Changed
- A row's hover actions are an icon and a key chip each, 24 high, shown at once for a hovered row and for the row
  the keyboard is on, and in the `Tab` order after it; an action that cannot run shows its icon `dim` and its key
  dashed (#235)

## Gaps
- A hover action held down has no pressed look in the design; the app gives it the press of a secondary button, the
  `stage` fill and an `ink` edge (#235)
- The hover actions' Snooze stays unavailable until the time picker opens from a row; its key chip shows dashed
  (#235)
- The mail list still draws its old rows: the hover actions stand over the row's first line on a `stage` patch until
  the rows cut their text short of them, and the error banner and the reader's banners keep their own layout until
  they move to the banner component (#235)
- A settings row whose control has the focus shows the row's focus edge and the control's own ring; the design shows
  the row's edge alone (#235)
- The running step of an account's check is the design's `[ .. ]` and stands still; the running mark of a progress
  line goes round. With motion off (not a level of its own yet) the ring will stand still and the bar give way to
  its words (#235)
