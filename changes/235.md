# 235

## Changelog
### Added
- The shared rows and blocks of the design as components: the list row (its hover fill by the ground it stands on,
  the current row's bar, the line under it, the focus edge inside it), the banner in its six kinds, the empty state,
  the progress line with its bar of ten characters, the settings row and the coming-soon one, a step of an account's
  check, and the facts of a message with their details (#235)
### Changed
- A row's hover actions are an icon and a key chip each, 24 high, on the row's first line, shown at once for a
  hovered row and for the row the keyboard is on, and in the `Tab` order after it; an action that cannot run shows
  its icon `dim` and its key dashed; a press on the row's middle opens the row at every width of the list (#235)
- The error banner is the design's banner: its ERROR label in `minus`, the words beside it and the `dismiss` link
  under them, in its place under the title strip or across the window as before (#235)
- Every focus ring stands on whole pixels at every scale: its line one point rounded to whole pixels (1 px at 125 %, 2 px at
  150 %), 2 points clear of its control; nothing moves at 100 % (#235)
- Text laid out through the components' paint helpers stands on the whole pixel nearest the design's baseline, as
  measured at 100 %: a caption line (Plex Sans 12/18), whose baseline falls half way between two pixels, goes one
  pixel up; each row of wrapped or centred text starts where the design's text box puts it (#235)
### Security
- Bidi embeddings, overrides and isolates in a mail's words are left out of what banners, facts, check steps and
  progress lines draw and of every name they give, so a message cannot turn its own words round; the direction marks
  stay, so a right-to-left name keeps its brackets and digits in place (#235)

## Gaps
- A hover action held down has no pressed look in the design; the app gives it the press of a secondary button, the
  `stage` fill and an `ink` edge (#235)
- The hover actions' Snooze cannot run yet: it shows its icon `dim` and its key H dashed, and runs nothing, until the
  time picker opens from a row (#235)
- The mail list still draws its old rows: the hover actions stand at the right end of the row's first line, right of
  its middle (what does not fit gives way from the end of the list's order, Snooze first, then Delete and Read, Done
  last; beside the reader Snooze is left out); a hovered row hides its time, the keyed row keeps it and its actions
  stand left of it, so at the list's default width the keyed row also gives up Delete (Done and Read show, Delete
  stays on its key); the reader's banners keep their own layout until they move to the banner component (#235)
- The design draws no flagged or pinned row under the pointer: the app keeps the flag and the pin on the first line,
  8 left of the hover actions and 8 apart, and cuts the sender 4 short of them (#235)
- A settings row whose control has the focus shows the row's focus edge and the control's own ring; the design shows
  the row's edge alone (#235)
- A banner's link is drawn whole while it fits the banner's room; the design's banner board cuts the second link of
  the remote-pictures banner (`always for news@brightshelf.example.org`) at 240 wide with an ellipsis, though the
  room would hold it (#235)
- egui draws a glyph on whole pixel rows only: a caption line stands 0.30 to 0.40 px above the design's place, whose
  baseline falls half way between two pixels (it stood 0.60 to 0.70 px under it) (#235)
- Text drawn by egui's own widgets (`ui.label` and the like: the list pane's screener note and the sidebar's
  addresses, the settings dialog's lines) keeps egui's baseline, so at 100 % it stands 1 px under caption text drawn
  through the components, until those views move onto the components in the lists, reader and screens slices (#235)
- At 125 % the second row of a wrapped run can land about 1 px off the design's line; the whole-pixel baseline is
  measured at 100 % (#235)
- The running step of an account's check is the design's `[ .. ]` and stands still; the running mark of a progress
  line goes round. With motion off (not a level of its own yet) the ring will stand still and the bar give way to
  its words (#235)
