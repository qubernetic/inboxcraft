# 370

## Changelog
### Added
- Settings › Calendar › Editor › New event opens chooses where the event editor opens, for a new event and for Edit
  alike (`ui.layout.set`'s new `event_editor`: `small`, `full` or `window`, kept between runs): the small panel over
  the calendar; the full editor in the calendar's place, its day's hours 320 wide beside it, following the day its
  Starts names (the default); or a window of its own, 704 × 800, its title the event's. The full editor and the
  window put Starts, Ends and the two ticks on one row and Repeat, Show as and Alert on another; Alert, the event's
  alarm, shows there only. Both give the keyboard to the title when they open. Where no second window can open (the
  web app), the editor opens full and the status line says why, once (#370)
### Changed
- The window's Esc is the editor's: with typing it warns, and a second throws the typing away. The desktop's close
  and the window's Close never throw typing away: with nothing typed they close the editor, otherwise the window
  stays and says how to keep or throw away the typing (#370)
- While the event editor is open in any of its hosts, the owner's Undo rests, as an agent's `history.undo` is refused
  (#370)
## Gaps
- The full editor and the event editor's window have no Place field and no buttons at the foot that move the editor
  to the other two hosts; the small editor has no "more room" link; the `event-editor-full` and `-window` frames draw
  them (#370).
- The description in the full editor and the window grows from 96 to 240 high as in the small editor; the frames give
  it the room left under the fields (314 and 258 high) (#370).
- The event editor's window has no status line of its own ("not saved yet · into Studio" and the cancel key in the
  `event-editor-window` frames) (#370).
- The day beside the full editor shows the events of the days the calendar has read (the view's): a Starts typed far
  from them shows that day's hours without its events; the draft's own block (`draft-picked` in the frames) is not
  drawn in it (#370).
- The event editor's window is not compared with the `event-editor-window` frames pixel for pixel: no picture draws a
  second window (#370).
