## Changelog
### Added
- A field's caret is the design's 7 × 16 block in the accent, blinking once a second while the field has the
  keyboard (#234)
- The rows of a menu or a picker come in one after the other, 30 ms apart, at full motion; with reduced motion they
  are all there at once (#234)
- Home and End go to the first and the last row of a menu (#234)
- A one-line field holds at most 4 096 characters (a password 256, a typed time 64), a field of several lines 64 KiB
  unless the screen that shows it names more, and the composer's message text 1 MiB; typing, Enter, a paste, a
  middle-click paste, an input method's text, an undo or a screen reader's edit that would make the text longer than
  that is refused whole, in whatever order a frame brings them: the text, the caret and the selection stay as they
  were (a refused input method's commit also takes its unfinished word out, as ending it does) and the field says it
  is too long, brought into view; a text already longer (a draft from the store) opens whole, can always be edited
  down and undone back up to the size it opened with, and never grows past that (#234)
- The Snooze of a row's hover actions opens the time picker under it, by the pointer or by Enter or Space; the time
  chosen snoozes that message, and Cancel or Esc closes the picker and gives the row the keyboard back (#234)
- `ui_shot` draws the fields, pickers and menus on a page of the gallery of their own (`"fields": true`), and opens
  a menu with `"menu"` (`more`, `move`, `from`, `templates`, `group`) (#234)
### Changed
- Text fields, multiline fields and password fields follow the design in every state: the label over a 36 high box,
  the edge `cyan_edge` with the focus and `minus` with an error, the error under the box; a multiline field grows
  from 96 to 240 high and then scrolls; a stored password shows no dots and says that it is kept in the system's
  credential store (#234)
- Menus, choice rows, the recipient suggestions, the typed time and the time picker follow the design in every state;
  every menu and picker (the reader's More and Move, the composer's From and Templates, a row's group menu, the
  selects, the time pickers) hangs 4 px under what opened it, keeps 3 px from the window's edge and scrolls when the
  window is smaller and keeps Tab inside itself; Esc closes it and gives the keyboard back to what opened it, a menu
  opened from the keyboard gives it back however it closes, and one opened by the pointer leaves it with nothing, so
  the window's keys act; one opened from another closes first (#234)
- A recipient line of the composer is the design's: its name in capitals, chips on `grid` with their cross, a chip
  that is not an address marked with a warning and named once, under the line, "Cc Bcc" at the right end; past four
  rows of chips the line scrolls, and Tab brings every chip's cross into view (#234)
- The add-account form puts the user name beside the password (#234)
### Fixed
- A key held on a row of a select's choices no longer opens them again, a select whose choice is gone from its
  options opens on its first row, and a screen reader's click on an open select closes it (#234)

## Gaps
- Fields, menus and pickers have looks the design does not draw yet, and the app chooses them: a pressed menu row,
  choice, suggestion or recipient chip takes the press of a secondary button (`stage` and an `ink` edge), a pressed
  chosen row or ticked choice the accent wash with the `cyan_edge` edge; a focused empty text field puts its hint
  after the caret as the typed time does; the caret stands 2 after the character before it, over the next one when
  it is inside the text (#234)
- A menu row from a plugin is drawn coming soon, without the plugin's tag; the time picker reads a date that has
  passed only when it is written with its year (`2026-11-02 09:00`), as `2 nov` always means the next one (#234)
- A long value in a one-line field is clipped at the box's right edge, not cut with an ellipsis; a caret moved into
  the 8 px above or below a scrolling multiline field's text scrolls it into the text's room (#234)
- While a screen reader listens, egui hands every glyph of a text to it every frame: the composer's message text
  costs about 2 ms a frame at 256 KiB and 12 ms at 1 MiB at rest, 3 and 18 ms while typing, over the budget of 4 ms
  near its 1 MiB cap (a field of several lines holds 64 KiB, under 1 ms); without one it costs under 0.5 ms at rest
  and 3.1 to 4.3 ms a frame typing at 1 MiB (p95 over three runs on a busy machine), at or over the budget. Typing
  in the composer costs about 12 ms a character at any size besides, outside the field, most likely the draft kept on
  every change (measured on a fast laptop, `ui_bench --compose`; #243) (#234)
- A frame with an undo or a redo in a field copies the field's whole text once more, into the field's own record of
  the frame's changes, so that a refused frame can be put back; other frames copy nothing of it (#234)
- egui copies a field's whole text every frame it is drawn, and twice more while it has the keyboard, a password's
  too, and frees those copies without wiping them; the field wipes its own copies of a password, but egui's are out
  of its reach (#234)
- The composer says a refused paste over its message text, and brings that line into view, so a person typing at
  the end of a long draft sees the page move up to it (#234)
- A field refuses a frame's input as a whole: when anything in a frame would take the text past its limit, all of
  that frame's change to the field goes, so a character typed in the same frame as a paste that is too long goes too
  and is typed again. The limit of a text opened over its cap is the size it opened with until the text changes
  from outside, drawn or not in between; such a change also clears the field's undo history, so Ctrl Z never brings
  back the text before it. A change from outside that keeps the length is not seen: the older, larger limit and the
  history stay. When something else moved the selection inside an unfinished input method word, a
  refused commit leaves that word in the text (until the next word of the input method takes its place). If egui
  has already used its second pass in that frame, the "too long" line shows one frame late (#234)
- The Sending page shows a stored signature whole; the account itself still cuts a signature at 2 000 characters
  when it is read from the store or saved, so a longer one written into the store by hand is cut there, before any
  field sees it (#234)
- A recipient line stops growing at four rows of chips and scrolls; the design's line grows to hold them all, with
  no limit drawn (#234)
- A row's group menu opens by the secondary press only, as before; there is no key for it (Shift F10 or the menu key
  would do; the list's slice) (#234)
- The composer's subject and message text are plain edits without the field's box until the composer is restyled
  (#234)
- A time typed or picked for a date past a change of daylight saving time is read on today's offset, an hour off
  (#234)
