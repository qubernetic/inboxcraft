# 397

## Changelog
### Added
- The command line with nothing typed lists GO TO (the inbox with how many messages it holds to read, New senders
  with how many wait, the calendar with the next event of the day) and LAST COMMANDS, the three commands run from
  the line most recently, newest first, each with its key or why it cannot run now (#397)
- A search typed in the command line lists, from any place, the newest three messages found with how many there
  are, the people the mail knows and the mailboxes whose path holds the text, each group under its head; the arrows
  move through all of them, and Enter or a press opens the message (in the mail searched for the text), the
  person's mail or the mailbox. A search that finds nothing says so, and Enter then shows the mail searched (#397)
### Changed
- A command chosen in the command line where it cannot run says in the status line why it did not run and what it
  needs, as well as in its row (#397)
- The command line's results end in the bar of prefixes, 30 high on the panel colour under its hairline (#397)

## Gaps
- Command line (#397): a refused command's reason shows in the status line as a notice that goes after its seconds,
  where the frame `command-line/refused` draws the error state; the words are the window's own ("Not run: Snooze or
  remind me needs an open message. Open one, then try again."), the row's full name where the frame says
  "Snooze". LAST COMMANDS holds the commands of this run of the app only, at most three (the frame draws two); GO
  TO always lists the inbox, New senders and the calendar, and with nothing to say leaves a caption out. With the
  line narrowed to the mailbox shown (`Ctrl F`) it lists no results but the prefixes, as before. The MAIL head's
  count is of the messages the list's search finds, which reads at most the engine's list limit per field; the
  PEOPLE count is of at most six people, the most the lookup keeps. The refused state is not among the pictures.
