# 271

## Changelog
### Added
- The calendar's side panel syncs the calendars on the owner's press: "sync now" at its foot runs `calendar.sync`
  and the line shows when the calendars were last read, the sync as it goes (the account read and a bar of the
  calendars done), the time it ended, or what failed in the window's words with "try again". A press while a sync
  runs starts no second one; nothing else (opening the calendar, a hover, the keyboard's focus, time passing) runs
  it. The line is one Tab stop that keeps the keyboard through its states, and it rests under the event editor (#271)

## Gaps
- Calendar sync line (#271): the time shown is the last read the window heard since it started, as hours and
  minutes without a date; the engine keeps no last-read time, so after a start the line says "calendars not read
  yet" until the start's own sync brings a calendar. "open log" under a failure is drawn coming soon without its tag
  (the 222-point line has no room for it): there is no log to open. A failure names the first account that failed,
  not how many did. Synced keeps its full bar until the next press, as the frame draws it, without the "sync now"
  link; the line itself takes the press. The foot keeps its 40 and 58 points at 130 % text.
