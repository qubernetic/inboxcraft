# 236

## Changelog
### Changed
- Every mail list (the cards and the grouped list of the inbox, the simple list, a folder, Later, search, New senders,
  the outbox) is laid out as lines of fixed height and builds only the lines in sight and two above and below: 10 000
  rows build about 20 lines a frame, and the grouped inbox's frame (p95) takes 0.35 ms where it took 0.51 (#236)
- A group's head and its "Show all" line are the group head's 32 points high (at the text scale), on the cards and
  in the grouped list alike (#236)
- When a group opens or folds above the rows in sight, or mail arrives above them, the first message in sight stays
  where it was; at the top of the list the new mail shows at the top (#236)
- The row the keyboard moves to is scrolled into sight, built or not; Page Down and Page Up move the keyboard's row
  by the rows in sight, End and Home to the last and the first row (#236)
- A screen reader is told each row's place in the whole list and the list's size, though only the rows in sight are
  in its tree (#236)
### Fixed
- A short simple list no longer shows a scroll thumb beside rows that all fit (#236)

## Gaps
- A screen reader sees only the rows in sight (and two above and below) as items; the rest of the list is there as
  its size and each row's place in it, not as items it can move to without scrolling (#236)
- The arrow keys step through the inbox in the list's order, a folded card's hidden rows included: the keyboard's
  row can be one its card does not show, and then nothing scrolls to it (#236)
- `Tab` walks the rows built: past the last row in sight the focus leaves the list instead of going on down it (#236)
- The engine lists at most 1 000 messages of a place; the list says "1000 messages" and shows no more (#236)
