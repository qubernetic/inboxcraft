## Changelog
### Added
- Three read-only MCP tools: `mail_why` (`mail.why`, by message `id`) says why a message stands where it does, at
  most four reasons a line with the sender's address quoted as untrusted; `week_settings` (`week.settings`, no
  arguments) says how weeks are counted and which week today is; `sync_status` (`sync.status`, no arguments) says
  how syncing stands, and reads `offline` on a store, where no account syncs. All three run headless on a store
  and in the running app (#273)
