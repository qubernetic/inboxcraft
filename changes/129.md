# 129

## Changelog
### Added
- The time picker, one component for snooze, send later and reminders: the presets still ahead with their time, a
  field whose text counts as soon as it reads as a time (`thu 9`, `12 nov 17:00`, `tomorrow`, `in 3 days`), without
  Enter, the time read shown under it and used by the confirm button, and an alert switch that keeps its last
  value; in the component gallery, and the typed time has the fuzz target `ui_typed_time` (#129)
### Changed
- The switch of the settings sheet is a component of its own (`component::switch`), shared with the time picker
  (#129)

## Roadmap
- The shared time picker for snooze, send later and reminders, with typed times, in the component gallery (#129)

## Gaps
- The time picker reads typed times in English words only and on one fixed offset from UTC: a time typed across a
  daylight saving change is off by the change, and the app still passes UTC until the computer's offset is read.
  The presets come from the caller; the scheduler's presets join with the snooze and send later screens. The
  alert switch keeps its value while the app runs, not between runs (#129).
