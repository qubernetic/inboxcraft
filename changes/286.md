# 286

## Changelog
### Added
- The command line's sets, not yet behind a screen: the command field (the prompt, the scope's mark and name for
  commands, people, mailboxes, one mailbox or all mail, the text with its caret or the hint, and the key chip that
  shows the opening key at rest and Esc once the field is in use; it holds at most 1 000 characters and cuts a
  longer paste where it goes in), the key hint (a key chip and the word of its action, information without a Tab
  stop, its click the press) and the window's own controls (minimise, maximise or restore, close; each a Tab stop
  pressed by Enter and Space). `ui_shot`'s `command_sets` draws every state of the three on one page (#286)

## Gaps
- Command field (#286): with the focus a text longer than the field scrolls to the caret instead of being cut with
  `…` at its end as the `long` frame draws it; without the focus it is cut with one `…`. An empty field of commands,
  people or mailboxes shows no hint (the sheet draws none). A mailbox's name in the scope mark keeps to a third of
  the field and is cut with `…` (the sheet gives it no bound). The field keeps the 1 px edge and the 13 points to
  its text at 130 % text; its mark is 20 high there and the caret 9 × 20.
- Window controls (#286): the sheet draws no pressed state and no focus ring; a focused cell shows the focus ring
  inside its 36 × 32 cell. At 130 % text a cell is 36 × 42 and its glyph 20.
