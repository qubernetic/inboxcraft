# 146

## Changelog
### Added
- The calendar commands: `calendar.list` (`safe`) lists the calendars of every account (or one) with their events
  counted, `event.list` (`safe`) the occurrences of their events in a span of up to 400 days, repeating events
  expanded, each with its moments in UTC and on the computer's wall clock, its first and last day, all-day, busy or
  free, private, its repeat preset (or `custom`), its guests with their answers, and the event's own zone with its
  wall-clock times when that clock shows another time than the computer's; an event whose text cannot be read is
  counted, not shown (#146)
- `event.create`, `event.update` and `event.delete` (`owner`): an event with a summary, a place, a description, a
  repeat preset (`event.repeats`: none, daily, weekdays, weekly, monthly, yearly), all-day or timed, busy or free,
  private and guests, kept at once and written to the server (`Put` new, or over the copy seen); an edit keeps the
  event's own zone, and tells the guests (`SEQUENCE` counted on) only with `notify` (the default is not to). A delete
  takes the event away at once and goes to the server once the six-second undo window is over; `history.undo` takes
  a delete back within the window, and a new event or an edit back at any time (#146)
- `calendar.sync` (`safe`) finds the calendars at each account's CalDAV address and brings them in step, as the start
  does; `calendar.server` (`owner`) sets or clears an account's CalDAV address, `https` only (#146)
### Changed
- The engine applies the calendar reports it dropped before: calendars and events keep their ids by their URLs, the
  sync token is kept, a written event takes its new entity tag, and a conflict (412) syncs the calendar afresh (#146)
- `Session::next_due` and `Session::tick` also cover a held delete (#146)

## Roadmap
- E4.4 and E4.5 in the engine: the calendars and their events listed with recurrence expanded and the event's own
  zone, events made, changed and deleted with repeat presets, all-day, busy or free, private and guests, a delete
  held for the undo window, undo, the CalDAV reports applied (#146)

## Gaps
- A new event is written in UTC, a repeating one on the computer's wall clock (floating), never in a named zone: the
  engine knows the computer's offsets, not its zone's name or rules, so it writes no `VTIMEZONE`. A repeating event
  shared with guests elsewhere shows at their own wall-clock time (#146).
- Changing or deleting a repeating event changes or deletes the whole series: no single instance (`RECURRENCE-ID`,
  `EXDATE`) is edited or deleted, changed instances stay as they were when the series moves, and a repeat preset
  replaces the series' `RDATE`s (#146).
- `notify` only counts `SEQUENCE` on, and a new event's guests are only named in it: nothing is mailed to them until
  the invitations send iTIP over iMIP. Changing an event someone else organises changes the copy on the owner's
  server only (#146).
- An edit or delete the server refuses because the event changed elsewhere (412) is replaced by the server's copy at
  the full sync the conflict starts: the owner's change is lost, with no notice yet. A failed calendar job is not
  shown to the owner (#146).
- Undoing a new event whose write is still on its way can reach the server before the write, which then makes the
  event again; it stays there until a full sync or the owner deletes it (#146).
- A delete within its undo window when InboxCraft quits goes to the server at the next start, without the window
  (#146).
- `event.list` answers at most 1000 occurrences, and a discovery keeps at most 100 calendars of an account (#146).
