# 143

## Changelog
### Added
- `inboxcraft-ical`, iCalendar (RFC 5545): calendar objects read into a tree of components, properties and
  parameters (lines unfolded on the bytes, RFC 6868 parameter escapes) and written back with CRLF, lines folded at
  75 octets and CR, LF and NUL left out of values; typed values (dates, the three forms of date and time, durations,
  UTC offsets, TEXT escapes); recurrence rules read, written and expanded on the wall clock with every rule part
  (`BYSETPOS`, `BYWEEKNO`, `WKST`, ordinal weekdays, days a month does not have skipped); `VTIMEZONE`s as `time`'s
  `Offsets`; events with their people, answers, alarms, `RDATE`, `EXDATE` and changed instances
  (`RECURRENCE-ID`), and the occurrences of a calendar in a span of time, summer time followed. Capped: 4 MiB,
  50 000 properties and components, nesting 8 deep, 10 000 occurrences per span; property tests for recurrence,
  round trips and spans; the fuzz targets `ical_calendar` and `ical_rule` (#143)

## Roadmap
- E4.1: `ical` reads and writes iCalendar, time zones from `VTIMEZONE`, recurrence expanded with property tests,
  two fuzz targets (#143)

## Gaps
- `ical` has no IANA zone data: a `TZID` without a `VTIMEZONE` in the same calendar object is read on the owner's
  own wall clock (UTC's own names excepted), and `ical` cannot write a `VTIMEZONE` for a zone it has not read. A
  UTC offset's seconds are dropped (#143).
- Recurrence: `DTSTART` is always the first instance, also where the rule would not make it, and `COUNT` counts the
  rule's own occurrences; `RECURRENCE-ID;RANGE=THISANDFUTURE` changes only its own instance; an `RDATE` period
  counts by its start, its own end dropped; second 60 reads as 59. An expansion stops after 100 000 empty periods
  in a row, 100 000 candidates in one period, 1 000 000 occurrences walked through or 10 000 given, and says so
  (#143).
- `Event` keeps the properties and components it does not read, but parameters it does not read on the properties
  it reads (`ALTREP` or `LANGUAGE` on `SUMMARY`, an `X-` parameter on `DTSTART`) are lost when an event is written
  from its fields; the component tree keeps everything. `VTODO`, `VJOURNAL` and `VFREEBUSY` are kept unread (#143).
