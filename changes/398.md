# 398

## Changelog
### Changed
- An account's row in the index says whether it is syncing, offline or had its sign-in refused, read from
  `sync.status` and `account.list` when the mailbox changes or a second has passed; it was always quiet (#398)
- A folder under another is named in the index by its path's parts with " / " between them ("Clients / Harbour
  Bakery"), as the design file draws it, not by the server's path with its own delimiter (#398)
### Fixed
- The index's rows stand on whole pixels at fractional scales: at 125 % a 34-point row is 42.5 pixels, and its fill,
  bar and focus edge were smeared over half a pixel (#398)
## Gaps
- The index shows a Junk row, and a Blocked row while InboxCraft's Blocked folder exists, which the frames do not
  draw (`index/with-counts`: Inbox, New senders, Drafts, Outbox, Sent, Archive, Bin): their mail has no other place
  (#398).
- `sync.status` says whether a sync round runs, not whose: while one account syncs, every account signed in and not
  failing says syncing, and while another account fails, one in its first round says so only once a worker has told
  its progress (#398).
- At 130 % New message is 42 points high, 52.5 pixels at 125 %: the button is not on whole pixels as the index's
  rows are (#398).
