# 348

## Changelog
### Added
- Hebrew, Arabic, Devanagari and Thai text and emoji are drawn with fonts that ship with the app (Noto Sans
  Hebrew, Arabic, Devanagari and Thai and the one-colour Noto Emoji, SIL Open Font Licence 1.1, Regular only)
  behind IBM Plex in every text style, so they no longer depend on the computer's fonts; the icons keep Phosphor
  alone. The desktop app grows by about 1.2 MiB (#348)
- At start the computer's fonts are searched for 21 scripts the app's fonts lack: Chinese, Japanese, Korean and
  Bengali, Gurmukhi, Gujarati, Oriya, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Sinhala, Lao, Tibetan, Myanmar, Khmer,
  Ethiopic, Syriac, Thaana, Cherokee and Bopomofo, keeping at most 24 faces and 96 MiB; the faces found stand behind
  the shipped ones (#348)

## Gaps
- The Flatpak does not install the fonts' licence texts (SIL Open Font Licence 1.1 for Noto, IBM Plex and
  Audiowide) under `/app/share/licenses/`; the notices travel only in the fonts' own name tables (#348, #335).
