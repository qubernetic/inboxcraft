# 113

## Changelog
### Added
- The store's schema version 4 with every local table the daily-use features need: the job queue, drafts, the
  outbox, the schedule, senders (known, the screener's decision, the group), each message's own state (done,
  parked, pinned, snoozed, follow-up) and the calendars with their sync token, events (ETag and iCalendar text) and
  alerts; their records in `model` (`Draft`, `Outgoing`, `Scheduled`, `Sender`, `MessageState`, `Calendar`,
  `CalendarEvent`, `Alert`) and their `Change` variants in both backends (#113)
- Kept jobs: `Outcome::with_kept_job` writes a job into the store's queue in the same batch as the command's
  changes; it leaves the queue with its last event, and `Session::start` hands the jobs an earlier run left over
  again (#113)
### Changed
- `inboxcraft` keeps the owner's mailbox on disk, in `inboxcraft/store` in the data folder (`XDG_DATA_HOME`, in
  the Flatpak `~/.var/app/io.github.qubernetic.inboxcraft/data`), starts the session at once (left-over jobs, then
  the folder lists) and no longer removes the accounts' passwords when the window closes; without a data folder,
  or with a store that does not open, it says why and does not open the window (#113)

## Roadmap
- The desktop app keeps its store on disk; schema version 4 holds the job queue, drafts, outbox, schedule,
  senders, message state and calendars; kept jobs survive a restart (#113)

## Gaps
- `store` keeps the schedule without an account and senders across accounts: removing an account leaves its
  schedule entries, and the engine drops an entry whose draft, message or alert is gone when it is due. Schedule
  entries and alerts are ordered by the second they are due, then id; the fraction of a second is kept but not
  sorted on. Senders are told apart by the address in Unicode lower case, as `EmailAddress` compares them (#113).
- Two `inboxcraft` windows started at once open the same store; until the single instance guard comes (A1), the
  second one's sweep at open can remove a source file the first one has written but not committed yet, which the
  first then reports as `Corrupt` (#113).
- A kept job runs again after a restart under its old id, also when its worker had already run it before the app
  ended: kept jobs are for acts that can be done twice (#113).
